Returns & Exchanges

Last updated: September 2026

Return Policy

We want you to love your DRAPE purchase. If something isn't right, we're here to help.

7-day return window: You can return items within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.

Conditions:

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition
  • Tags must still be attached
  • Items must be in original packaging

How to Return

Step 1: Contact us via WhatsApp or email with your order ID and reason for return.

Step 2: We'll confirm eligibility and send you return instructions.

Step 3: Pack the item securely and drop off at our designated courier point (free for Dhaka customers).

Step 4: Refund processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the return.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Contact us within 7 days and we'll arrange an exchange subject to availability.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Accessories (jewellery, bags) — unless defective
  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Custom or personalized orders

Defective Items

If you receive a defective or damaged item, contact us within 48 hours with photos. We'll arrange a free pickup and full refund or replacement.
